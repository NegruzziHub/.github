# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

NegruzziHub is a club made up of students of Colegiul Național "Costache Negruzzi" Iași, with a shared interest in building things together. Some of us are just starting out, some of us have been coding for years : that gap should never be a reason for anyone to feel unwelcome.

This is the organization-wide default. It applies to every NegruzziHub repository unless a specific project defines its own, more specific version.

## What we expect

- **Be patient with beginners.** Everyone asking a question today will be answering someone else's question next year.
- **Give feedback on the work, not the person.** "This function could be simpler" is useful. "This is bad code" isn't.
- **Assume good intent.** Most misunderstandings online come from tone getting lost in text, not from actual malice.
- **Respect people's time.** Most of us are also balancing school : don't expect an instant response.
- **Credit people's work.** If you build on someone else's code or idea, say so.

## What we don't tolerate

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks : in issues, pull requests, commit messages, or anywhere else connected to the club
- Discriminatory language or behavior of any kind
- Deliberately submitting broken or malicious code
- Sharing someone else's private information without their permission
- Taking credit for work that isn't yours

## Where this applies

Across all NegruzziHub repositories, issues, and pull requests, and in any club-affiliated chat or in-person meeting.

## If something happens

Reach out to current club leadership or a coordinating teacher at Colegiul Național "Costache Negruzzi" Iași. Reports are taken seriously and handled with discretion.

## A final note

We're a small club, we mostly know each other, and how we treat one another here reflects on all of us : the club and the school both.
